Our Story

A neighborhood bakery with a slow-food heart.

The Sugar Magnolia opened in 2016, when baker Hannah Cole turned her farmers-market stall into a little storefront on Ponce de Leon. Everything here is made in small batches — our sourdough ferments for 24 hours, our butter is cultured, and our cakes are built to order.

We bake what we love and sell it the same day. When the case is empty, we close the oven door and start again tomorrow.
