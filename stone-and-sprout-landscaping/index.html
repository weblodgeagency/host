Our Story

Built on Pittsburgh hillsides — literally.

Pittsburgh yards are tough: steep slopes, clay soil, and winters that test everything. We started Stone & Sprout in 2011 because we love the challenge. Our crews know how to terrace a hillside, drain a soggy yard, and pick plants that thrive in Pennsylvania soil.

We're a small team, and we like it that way. The person who quotes your job is the person who shows up to do it.
