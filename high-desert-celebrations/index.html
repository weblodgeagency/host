Our Studio

Rooted in New Mexico, made for your story.

High Desert Celebrations was founded in 2017 by Sofia Delgado, an Albuquerque planner who grew up at big family fiestas and never lost her love of a good gathering. We plan weddings, quinceañeras, and celebrations across New Mexico — from Old Town courtyards to ranches in the foothills.

Our style is warm and unpretentious: adobe and turquoise, marigolds and string lights, food that people actually want to eat. Every event we plan feels like the people throwing it — never like a template.
