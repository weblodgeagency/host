About Beacon

A law firm built for the way modern companies actually work.

Beacon Legal Group was founded in 2014 by three Silicon Valley attorneys who were tired of the old model: slow replies, surprise bills, and contracts nobody could read. We built a firm around the opposite — clear flat fees, same-week turnarounds, and lawyers who answer their own email.

Today we advise more than 600 companies across the Bay Area, from two-person startups to established firms with hundreds of employees.

  • Flat-fee pricing on most services — quoted up front
  • Same-week turnaround on standard contracts
  • Secure client portal for documents and messaging
  • Video calls available — no need to come downtown
