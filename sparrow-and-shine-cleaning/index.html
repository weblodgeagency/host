Our Story

A family business with a neighbor's touch.

Sparrow & Shine began in 2016 when sisters Ana and Rosa started cleaning homes in their own East Side neighborhood. Word spread, the team grew — but the rule stayed the same: treat every home like it's our own mother's.

We don't send strangers. You'll know your cleaners by name, and they'll know your home — where the dog likes to nap, which shelf holds the good china, and exactly how you like your pillows fluffed.
