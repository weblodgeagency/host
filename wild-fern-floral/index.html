Our Story

Grown nearby, arranged by hand, delivered with care.

Wild Fern opened in 2017 in a sunlit storefront on Fayetteville Street. We buy from North Carolina flower farms whenever we can, and every bouquet is arranged the morning it goes out — never pulled from a cooler shelf.

Our style is loose, garden-gathered, and a little bit wild: seasonal blooms, trailing greenery, and colors that look like they grew that way.
