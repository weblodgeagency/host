Meet Your Photographer

Hi, I'm Claire.

I'm a Seattle photographer who believes the best photos happen when nobody is posing. I photograph weddings, elopements, and families all around Puget Sound — from misty beach ceremonies on Bainbridge Island to golden-hour portraits in Discovery Park.

My approach is quiet and unobtrusive. I'll guide you when you need it and disappear when you don't, so your photos show the day as it really felt: joyful, a little wild, and completely yours.

“I don't want stiff photos. I want the laugh you didn't know I caught, and the look that says everything.”
