About Harborview

Grown in Maine, for Maine yards.

We started Harborview in 2015 with a pickup, a mower, and a love for this coastline. Portland yards face salt wind, rocky soil, and short growing seasons — so we plant tough, salt-tolerant natives and build stonework that shrugs off the winter.

"A yard should feel like it belongs here — because it does. We work with the coast, not against it."

— Ben Calloway, founder of Harborview Lawn & Garden
