Our Salon

Your neighborhood groomer.

Suds & Whiskers started in 2020 as a two-person shop in Sugar House, and we've grown the old-fashioned way: one happy pet at a time. We know most of our regulars by name — the pets and the people.

Our groomers are certified, patient, and genuinely love animals. We take extra time with nervous pets, senior dogs, and cats with strong opinions. No pet is ever rushed through.

  • Certified groomers, pet first-aid trained
  • Gentle, hypoallergenic products
  • Photo text updates during every groom
  • Loyalty card — every 10th groom is free
