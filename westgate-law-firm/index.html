About Westgate

A Fort Worth firm built on handshake values.

Westgate Law Firm was founded in 2004 by Tom Westgate, a Fort Worth native who believed legal help should feel like talking to a trusted neighbor — not a billing machine. That spirit still runs the firm today.

"We answer our own phones. We return calls the same day. And we never take a case we don't believe in."

From our office near Sundance Square, our team of seven attorneys serves families and small businesses across Tarrant County and beyond.
