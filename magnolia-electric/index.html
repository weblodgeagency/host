Our Story

Three generations of Jackson electricians.

Walter Hargrove started Magnolia Electric in 1987 with a pickup truck and a reputation for doing things right. Today his grandson runs the company the same way — licensed journeymen, honest quotes, and work we're proud to put our name on.

We specialize in the homes other electricians avoid: historic houses in Belhaven and Fondren, with plaster walls, old wiring, and details worth preserving. We rewire them carefully and leave them beautiful.
