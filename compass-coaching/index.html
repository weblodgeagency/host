How We Work

Tutoring is the map. Coaching is the compass.

Compass opened in 2020 in Homewood with a simple belief: students do best when they understand both the subject and themselves. Our tutors teach the material; our coaches build the habits — planning, focus, and confidence — that make learning stick.

Every student starts with a free consultation and a personal plan. Parents get a progress update after every session.
