About the Shop

A Lakeview shop since 2009

Lakefront Auto Care started with two bays, one lift, and a simple promise: treat every customer like a neighbor, because around here, you probably are one. Sixteen years later, we have grown to eight bays — but the promise has not changed.

Most of our customers come from referrals. That only happens when the work is honest and the price is fair, so that is what we do, every single day.
