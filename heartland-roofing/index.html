Our Story

A family business, built one roof at a time.

Heartland Roofing started in 2008 when brothers Dan and Rick Meyer decided Kansas City deserved a roofing company that answered the phone, showed up on time, and stood behind its work. Today their kids work alongside them — and the standards have not budged an inch.

We know the weather here: spring hail, summer storms, and the freeze-thaw cycles that wear a roof down year after year. Every roof we install is built for Missouri weather, and every job is backed by our 20-year workmanship warranty.
