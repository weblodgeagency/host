Our Story

A little flower shop with a big Southern heart.

Petal & Stem opened in 2015 in a former carriage house on Bull Street, two blocks from Forsyth Park. We buy from Georgia flower farms, arrange everything by hand, and believe a bouquet should look like it was just gathered — not assembled.

From anniversary roses to full wedding weekends in the Historic District, we pour the same care into every stem.
