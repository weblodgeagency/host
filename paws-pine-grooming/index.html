Our Studio

No cages. No rush. No stressed pets.

Paws & Pine opened in 2019 because we believed grooming could be calmer. Our studio is cage-free, softly lit, and quiet — one groomer, one pet, one unhurried appointment at a time.

We work with nervous dogs, senior pets, and cats who have strong opinions about baths. Every pet gets patience, treats, and plenty of breaks. If your pet needs extra time, they get it — no charge, no fuss.
