About Desert Current

Tucson electricians who plan for the sun.

We started Desert Current in 2014 because Tucson homes deserve electricians who understand the desert — the heat, the monsoon storms, and the solar panels on half the roofs in town.

Every job is done by a licensed journeyman, permitted where required, and backed by a 3-year warranty. We show up on time, explain the work in plain English, and clean up before we leave.
