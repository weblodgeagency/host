About the Firm

A Tampa firm that treats you like a neighbor, not a case number.

James Harrington and Rachel Cole founded this firm in 1999 with a simple belief: good legal help should be clear, honest, and within reach. Today our team of nine attorneys serves clients across the Tampa Bay area from our downtown office on Kennedy Boulevard.

"Most people come to us during the hardest week of their lives. Our job is to make the legal part the easiest part."

We return every call within one business day, explain your options in plain English, and give you a written fee agreement before any work begins.
