Our Salon

A slower kind of salon.

Shear & Stone opened in 2017 with a simple idea: fewer chairs, longer appointments, and stylists who actually listen. No rushing, no upselling, no haircut that falls apart the moment you wash it at home.

We use ammonia-free color and cruelty-free products, and every service starts with a real conversation about your hair, your routine, and what you want to walk out with.

  • Free consultation with every service
  • Ammonia-free, cruelty-free color
  • Online booking, same-day slots
  • Gender-neutral pricing
