Meet Your Photographer

Hi, I'm Hannah.

I'm a Phoenix-based photographer who fell in love with desert light and never left. For eight years I've photographed weddings in the Sonoran Desert, engagements at golden hour, and families who just want beautiful photos without the awkward posing.

My style is warm, natural, and unposed — I guide you gently, then step back and let the real moments happen. The laugh, the tear, the look between you two: that's the photo.
