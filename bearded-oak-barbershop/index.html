Our Shop

Old-school craft, zero pretension.

The Bearded Oak started in 2015 with two chairs, a straight razor, and a simple rule: do the cut right or don't do it at all. Ten years later we have six chairs, the same rule, and a waiting list on Saturdays.

Every service ends with a hot towel and a proper neck shave. Coffee's on us, the conversation is free, and your barber will remember your cut next time you sit down.
