Our Story

A Tampa bakery with Cuban roots.

Sweetwater Bakehouse opened in 2018 in a sunny corner of downtown Tampa. Our founder, Marisol Vega, grew up baking in her grandmother's kitchen in Ybor City — and her guava pastelitos are still the first thing to sell out every day.

Alongside the Cuban classics, we bake slow-fermented breads, French pastries, and celebration cakes for Tampa's birthdays, weddings, and quinceañeras.
