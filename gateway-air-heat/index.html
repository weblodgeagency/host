About Gateway Air & Heat

Locally owned, St. Louis proud

Gateway Air & Heat started in 2013 with two vans and a belief that HVAC companies should be as reliable as the systems they install. Today we run ten trucks across the metro — but we still answer our own phones, and the owner still checks in on every install.

Our technicians are NATE-certified, background-checked, and paid by the job, not by commission. They will never sell you something you do not need.
