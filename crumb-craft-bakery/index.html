Our Story

Flour, water, salt, fire — and patience.

Crumb & Craft began in 2015 with a wood-fired oven and a belief that great bread doesn't need shortcuts. Our bakers mix by hand, ferment overnight, and bake in small batches through the morning.

We mill Virginia-grown grain in-house, source butter and eggs from nearby farms, and keep the menu short so everything on it is done right.
