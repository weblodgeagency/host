About North Peak

Local crew. Minnesota tough.

North Peak Roofing started in 2012 with one truck and a simple idea: a roofing company that shows up when it says it will, charges what it quoted, and cleans up after itself. Fourteen years later, we have roofed more than 1,400 homes across the Twin Cities — and most of our new jobs still come from neighbors recommending us.

We know Minnesota roofs: ice dams, hail, freeze-thaw cycles, and the summer storms that roll in off the prairie. Every roof we install is built for all of it.

  • Licensed & insured in MN
  • Own trained crew, no day labor
  • 25-year workmanship warranty
