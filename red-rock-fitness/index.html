Our Story

A neighborhood gym, built by neighbors.

Red Rock Fitness Co. started in 2015 when two San Antonio families got tired of big-box gyms where nobody knew your name. We wanted a place where the front desk greets you, the coaches correct your form, and the kids are welcome too.

Today we're one of the most-loved gyms in San Antonio — with 60+ classes a week, a full strength and cardio floor, and a kids' club that children actually ask to visit.
