About Pacific Strength

A gym for people who actually like training.

We opened Pacific Strength in 2017 because Long Beach needed a gym that took lifting seriously without taking itself too seriously. No mirrors everywhere, no waiting for a squat rack, no upselling — just good equipment, good coaching, and good people.

Our 10,000-square-foot facility has eight lifting platforms, a full dumbbell range up to 150 lbs, sled turf, and a dedicated conditioning area. Beginners get a free onboarding session so day one never feels overwhelming.

Classes & Training

Find your session

Every class is capped at 14 people and led by a certified coach. Your first class is free — just show up 10 minutes early.

Barbell Club

Structured strength programming around squat, bench, and deadlift. Lift in small groups with a coach checking every set.

60 min · All levels

Pacific Conditioning

Intervals, sleds, and bodyweight work in a fast 45 minutes. The class that gets your heart rate up and your week started right.

45 min · High energy

Olympic Lifting

Snatch and clean & jerk technique work with USAW-certified coaches. Platforms, bumpers, and plenty of patience.

75 min · Technique focused

Mobility & Recovery

Guided stretching, breathwork, and foam rolling to keep you lifting pain-free. Pairs perfectly with our sauna and cold plunge.

50 min · Low intensity
