Why Forge

A gym built around coaching, not machines.

Forge Fitness Studio opened in 2016 with one idea: a gym where every member has a plan and a coach who knows their name. No crowded floor, no guessing what to do next — just structured training that actually works.

Our 8,000-square-foot space in downtown Orlando has a full strength floor, a turf zone for conditioning, and a dedicated room for small-group classes. Come in for a free week and see the difference coaching makes.

  • Coach-led sessions, every time
  • Open gym 5 AM – 10 PM daily
  • Nutrition guidance included

Programs

Train the way you want to train

Every program is led by a certified coach and capped at small numbers, so you get real attention in every session.

Strength Training

Barbell-first programming built around the big lifts. Learn proper form, follow a progressive plan, and watch your numbers climb week after week.

Small groups · All levels

Forge Conditioning

High-energy 45-minute classes mixing sleds, ropes, kettlebells, and intervals. The fastest way to build real-world fitness — and have fun doing it.

45 min · 12 spots per class

Personal Training

One-on-one coaching with a program built around your goals, your schedule, and your body. Ideal for beginners, athletes, and anyone coming back from injury.

1-on-1 · From $65/session
