Who We Are

Boston neighbors who know New England yards.

Greenhaven started in 2012 with one truck, two brothers, and a simple promise: show up on time and leave the yard better than we found it. Today our crews care for homes across Boston and the surrounding towns — and the promise hasn't changed.

We know what grows here and what doesn't. We plant for New England winters, mow for New England summers, and clean up every leaf in between.

  • Licensed and insured, with background-checked crews
  • Same crew on your yard every week — they learn it by heart
  • Eco-friendly treatments safe for kids and pets
  • Free on-site estimates, answered within one business day
