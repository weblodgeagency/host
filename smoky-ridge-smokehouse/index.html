Our Story

One pit, one fire, no shortcuts.

Smoky Ridge started in 2014 with a single offset smoker and a stubborn idea: real barbecue takes time. Our pitmaster still lights the oak fire at 4 AM, and nothing leaves the pit until it's ready — not when the clock says so.

Every sauce, slaw, and side is made in-house every morning. The beer is cold, the patio is big, and the brisket sells out most nights — so come hungry and come early.
