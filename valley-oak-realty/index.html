About Valley Oak

A family brokerage, not a corporate chain.

Valley Oak Realty was founded in 2008 by the Alvarez family, who have lived and worked in Fresno for three generations. We started with one office and a simple promise: treat every client like family, and tell them the truth — even when it's not what they want to hear.

"We don't chase volume. We build relationships — and most of our business still comes from past clients and their families."

Today our team of eight agents serves Fresno, Clovis, and the surrounding Central Valley communities.
