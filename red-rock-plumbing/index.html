Our Story

A local crew that knows New Mexico homes.

Red Rock started in 2004 with one truck and a simple promise: show up when we say we will, charge what we quoted, and leave the house cleaner than we found it. Twenty years later, that promise hasn't changed — we've just added more trucks.

Adobe walls, slab foundations, hard water, and old galvanized lines — we've seen what Albuquerque homes throw at plumbers, and we know how to fix it for good.
