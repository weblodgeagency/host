Our Story

From a small kitchen in Bologna to Tradd Street.

Osteria Lume opened in 2012, when the Ricci family brought their recipes from Bologna to Charleston. Our pasta is still rolled by hand every morning, our sauces simmer for hours, and our bread comes out of the oven warm all day long.

"Lume" means light — and that's what we try to serve: a bright, welcoming room, honest Italian cooking, and a meal that lingers a little longer than planned.
