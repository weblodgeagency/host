Our Story

Three generations around one stove.

The Copper Skillet started in 1998, when Rosa and Dale Whitfield turned their family recipes into a little twelve-table cafe on South Congress. Their daughter Maria runs the kitchen today, and the rules haven't changed: cook from scratch, source from Texas farms, and treat every guest like family.

Our wood-fired oven runs all day, our bread is baked in-house every morning, and the coffee is always on. Whether it's a weeknight dinner or a Sunday gathering, there's a table here with your name on it.
