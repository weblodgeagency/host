Our Studio

Thoughtful planning, rooted in nature.

Sage & Cedar was founded in 2018 by Megan Torres, a Tucson planner who loves desert light, wildflowers, and celebrations that feel like the people throwing them. We plan weddings and events across Southern Arizona — from Saguaro National Park to historic downtown venues.

We believe the best events aren't the most expensive ones. They're the ones where every detail feels considered, every guest feels welcome, and the hosts actually get to enjoy themselves.

One dedicated plannerThe same person from first call to last dance.
