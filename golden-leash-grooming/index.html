Our Story

A spa, not a production line.

The Golden Leash opened in 2018 in a sunny little shop on University Avenue. We wanted a grooming studio that felt like a spa — calm music, warm towels, and groomers who take the time to know each pet by name.

Seven years on, we groom more than 200 pets a week, and most of them come back every month. Many of our clients have been with us since the beginning — and we like to think that's because we treat every pet like our own.
