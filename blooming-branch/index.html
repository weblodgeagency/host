Our Story

Rooted in the Garden District since 2013.

The Blooming Branch began in a tiny Magazine Street storefront with a wheelbarrow of magnolias and a big idea: New Orleans deserves flowers as bold and joyful as the city itself. Today we're one of the Garden District's favorite florists — for Mardi Gras parties, French Quarter weddings, and everyday just-because bouquets.

We source from Louisiana growers first, arrange everything by hand, and deliver across the city the same day.
